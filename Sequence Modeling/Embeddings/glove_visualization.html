Step 1: Word Vectors Encode Meaning
Two similar words (like "ice" and "steam") have vectors pointing in similar directions, but context words help distinguish them.
Step 2: Vector Difference Captures Contrast
wi - wj gives us a vector that points from one word to another, encoding their semantic difference.
Step 3: Context Vector Measures Relevance
The dot product (wi - wj)T w̃k tells us how much the context word k helps distinguish between words i and j.
Step 4: Ratios Reflect Co-occurrence
If "solid" appears more with "ice" than "steam", the ratio Pik/Pjk > 1, and the dot product should be positive.
